I am a Ph.D. student in physics at Sungkyunkwan University. My research explores particle dark matter and the physics of the early universe, connecting particle theory with cosmological observations.

### Research interests

- **Dark matter:** relic density, sterile-neutrino dark matter, boosted dark matter, and dark-sector phenomenology
- **Early universe:** first-order phase transitions, baryogenesis, inflation, and dark radiation
